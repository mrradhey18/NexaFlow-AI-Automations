+ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Social Media Management plans.

What's included in the Reels? +
Each Reel includes scripting support, editing (basic to 4K depending on plan), trending music, captions, SEO-optimized hashtags, custom thumbnail, and direct posting on your Instagram account. Premium plans also include hooks, advanced animations, and subtitles.
Do I need to provide the video footage? +
For Reels that require your actual business footage (like product showcases or your team), yes — you'd send us the raw clips. For template-style or motion-graphics Reels, we create everything from scratch. We'll guide you clearly during onboarding.
How fast will I see results on Instagram? +
Most clients see noticeable growth in reach and engagement within 30–45 days of consistent posting. Instagram rewards accounts that post regularly — the more consistent you are, the faster the algorithm pushes your content to new audiences.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? +
Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade your plan anytime as your business grows. Downgrades can be done at the end of your current monthly cycle. Just WhatsApp us and we'll handle the change seamlessly.
Will you post on Facebook too? +
Our plans are Instagram-primary. Reels posted on Instagram can be automatically cross-posted to your connected Facebook page at no extra cost. Dedicated Facebook-only management is available on request as an add-on.
Is there a contract or lock-in period? +
No long-term lock-ins. Our plans are monthly. We're confident in our quality, so we don't need to trap you. You can cancel at any time — though most clients stay because they see real growth.
What kind of businesses do you work with? +
We work with all types of local businesses in Lucknow and across India — clinics, salons, gyms, restaurants, coaching centres, real estate agencies, retail shops, personal brands, and more. If you sell to local customers, we can grow your social media.
How do I get started? +
