What Is Google Business Profile?

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is a free tool from Google that allows businesses to manage their online presence across Google Search and Google Maps.

When someone searches for "dental clinic near me" or "best homeopathic doctor in Kanpur," Google shows a map with local business listings — this is the Local Pack or 3-Pack. The businesses that appear here get the majority of clicks and calls.

GBP Ranking Factors: What Google Uses

Google uses three primary factors to determine which businesses appear in the Local Pack:

1. Relevance

How well does your GBP match what the searcher is looking for? This is influenced by your business category, services listed, keywords in your business description, and overall profile completeness.

2. Distance

How far is your business from the searcher (or from the location specified in the search)? While you can't control your physical location, you can optimize your service area settings and use location-specific keywords.

3. Prominence

How well-known and reputable is your business? This is determined by review count, review rating, response rate, website authority, and how often your business information appears consistently across the web.

💡 Pro Tip

While you can't significantly influence the "Distance" factor, you have full control over "Relevance" and "Prominence" — and these two factors are where most clinics fail to optimize properly.

Photos & Videos: The Visual Ranking Signal

Businesses with photos receive 42% more requests for directions and 35% more click-throughs to their websites. For dental clinics and homeopathic practices, professional photos are non-negotiable.

Minimum Photo Requirements

  • Exterior photo — How to find your clinic from the street
  • Interior photos — Reception area, treatment rooms, waiting area
  • Team photos — Doctor and staff (builds trust)
  • Equipment photos — Show advanced dental/medical equipment
  • Logo — High-res PNG on white background
  • Cover photo — Best image of your clinic (1024×575 minimum)

Review Generation Strategy for Clinics

Google reviews are one of the strongest ranking signals for local search. More importantly, patients trust reviews — 88% of people trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations.

⚠️ Important Warning

Never buy fake reviews or incentivize patients to leave reviews in exchange for discounts. Google's review policies prohibit this, and violations can result in your GBP being suspended or removed.

The WhatsApp Review Generation System

The most effective review generation method for Indian clinics uses WhatsApp automation. Here's how we implement it for our clients:

  1. Day 0 (Post-Visit): Send a "Thank you for visiting" WhatsApp message with a direct review link
  2. Day 2 (Follow-up): Send a check-in message asking how they're feeling + gentle review reminder
  3. Day 7 (Final): Send useful health tips relevant to their treatment + final review request

GBP Posts: Signal Activity to Google

GBP Posts are short updates that appear directly on your Google profile and in search results. Regular posting signals to Google that your business is active — which is a ranking factor.

GBP Post Calendar for Clinics

Week Post Type Topic Example
Week 1 Offer/Update "Free dental consultation this week"
Week 2 Health Tip "5 signs you need a dental checkup"
Week 3 Event "Oral health camp on Saturday"
Week 4 Before/After "Patient smile transformation story"

Q&A Section: Seed It Yourself

The Q&A section on your GBP is indexed by Google. You should proactively add the most common questions patients ask — and answer them yourself. This helps your profile rank for question-based searches.

Example questions to add for a dental clinic:

  • Does your clinic offer dental implants?
  • Do you accept walk-in patients?
  • Is there parking available at your clinic?
  • What are your fees for a root canal?
  • Do you provide dental services for children?

Services & Products: Fill Every Field

GBP allows you to list your specific services with descriptions and prices. This is a major keyword opportunity that most clinics ignore. Each service you add creates additional ranking opportunities for service-specific searches.

For a dental clinic, list services such as: Teeth Cleaning, Root Canal Treatment, Dental Implants, Orthodontics, Teeth Whitening, Pediatric Dentistry, Emergency Dental Care, and Dental Crowns.

Include a brief keyword-rich description and price range for each service.

Tracking Your GBP Results

Use Google Business Profile Insights (available in your GBP dashboard) to track:

  • Profile Views: How many people saw your profile in Search and Maps
  • Search Queries: Which keywords brought people to your profile
  • Customer Actions: Calls, website clicks, direction requests
  • Photo Views: How often your photos are viewed vs competitors
