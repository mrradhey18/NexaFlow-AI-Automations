1 Why "Ranking on Google" Isn't Enough Anymore

Traditional SEO focused on one destination: Google's search results page. But AI search tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Bing Copilot) don't just crawl and rank pages — they synthesize an answer from multiple sources and decide which businesses are worth recommending by name.

This means visibility now depends on several signals working together, not any single one in isolation:

  • Structured, credible information across your website, directories, and business listings
  • Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across every platform an AI model might pull from
  • Genuine review volume and sentiment — AI models weigh review language, not just star ratings
  • Authoritative content that directly answers the questions your customers are asking — most AI citations actually come from off-site sources, not your homepage
  • Social proof and activity across Instagram, Facebook, and local business listings

Notice that a Google Business Profile isn't on that list by itself. GBP is one input among many — a useful one, but not the main pillar. We regularly onboard businesses that don't have a GBP set up at all, or have one that's never been touched. We build it as part of the system, alongside everything else — a business isn't invisible to AI just because a GBP is missing, but it is if all five signals above are missing.

FAQ? Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my business to show up in AI search results like ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?

AI models pull from your Google Business Profile, website content, reviews, and consistent local citations. Optimizing all of these together — not just your website — is what determines whether an AI tool recommends your business by name.

Is AI search optimization different from regular SEO?

It builds on regular SEO but goes further — AI models prioritize clearly structured, directly-answering content and consistent trust signals (reviews, citations, activity) over keyword density alone.

What if my business doesn't have a Google Business Profile yet?

That's a common starting point, not a blocker. We set up and verify your profile as one part of a wider system — GBP alone isn't what gets a business recommended by AI, so we build out your website content, citations, and review flow alongside it from day one.
