## What is Google Business Profile?

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is your **free business listing on Google**. When someone searches "dental clinic near me" or "best doctor in Kanpur," the businesses that appear in the map results are all using GBP.

Getting into the **Google Maps 3-Pack** (the top 3 results) can completely transform your business. Clinics we work with have seen 3x more phone calls within 60 days of proper optimization.

## Why GBP Matters for Your Clinic

Before a patient calls you, they search Google. Here is what they see:

- Your star rating and number of reviews
- Your photos and the quality of your profile
- Your address, hours, and services
- Whether you respond to reviews

A poorly optimized profile loses patients to competitors — even if your clinic is better.

## 15 Proven GBP Optimization Strategies

### 1. Choose the Right Primary Category

Your primary category is the single most important GBP ranking factor. For a dental clinic, use **"Dental Clinic"** not just "Health." Be as specific as possible.

### 2. Complete Every Section

Google rewards complete profiles. Fill in:
- Business name (exact legal name)
- Address and phone number
- Website URL
- Business hours (including holiday hours)
- Services with descriptions and prices
- Products if applicable

### 3. Upload High-Quality Photos

Profiles with photos receive **42% more requests for directions** and **35% more website clicks** according to Google's own data.

Upload:
- Exterior photos (front of clinic, signage)
- Interior photos (waiting room, treatment rooms)
- Team photos (doctors, staff)
- Equipment photos
- Before/after results (with patient permission)

### 4. Write a Keyword-Rich Business Description

Your description (up to 750 characters) should naturally include:
- Your primary service ("dental clinic," "homeopathic doctor")
- Your city ("in Kanpur," "in Lucknow")
- Your specialties

**Example:** "Sharma Dental Care is a leading dental clinic in Kanpur providing general dentistry, dental implants, orthodontics, and cosmetic dentistry. With 15+ years of experience, Dr. Sharma and team serve patients across Kanpur Nagar."

### 5. Get Google Reviews Consistently

Reviews are one of the top 3 GBP ranking factors. The key is **velocity** — getting reviews regularly, not just in bursts.

**Our WhatsApp review system:**
1. Patient visits → 24 hours later
2. Send personal WhatsApp message
3. Include direct review link
4. Follow up once if no response

This system generates 5–15x more reviews than asking verbally.

### 6. Respond to Every Review

Google sees review responses as engagement signals. Respond to:
- **Positive reviews** — thank them, mention a specific detail
- **Negative reviews** — apologize, offer to resolve, stay professional

### 7. Post Weekly Updates

GBP Posts keep your profile active. Post:
- Offers and promotions
- News and updates
- Events
- New services

Posts expire after 7 days for offers, so schedule weekly.

### 8. Enable Messaging

Turn on GBP Messaging so patients can message you directly from your profile. Respond within 24 hours to maintain your response rate.

### 9. Add All Your Services

List every service you offer with:
- Service name
- Description (include keywords naturally)
- Price range (optional but helpful)

### 10. Set Up Products (If Applicable)

If you sell products (dental products, medicines, etc.), add them to the Products section with photos and prices.

### 11. Answer Q&A Proactively

The Q&A section on your GBP is public. Add common questions yourself before patients ask them. This also adds keyword-rich content to your profile.

### 12. Use Google Business Profile Attributes

Attributes tell patients things like:
- "Wheelchair accessible"
- "Online appointments"
- "Accepts new patients"
- "Speaks Hindi"

These help you show up in filtered searches.

### 13. Verify Your Business

If you haven't verified your GBP, do it immediately. Unverified profiles rank poorly and cannot be fully managed.

### 14. Build Local Citations

Your NAP (Name, Address, Phone) must be **exactly the same** across:
- Justdial
- Practo
- Sulekha
- IndiaMart
- Your website

Inconsistencies hurt your local rankings.

### 15. Monitor Your Insights

Check GBP Insights monthly:
- How customers found you (direct vs discovery)
- What keywords triggered your listing
- Phone calls and direction requests

Use this data to improve your strategy.

## Common GBP Mistakes to Avoid

- Using a different business name than your signage
- Listing a fake or virtual address
- Keyword stuffing in your business name
- Not responding to negative reviews
- Uploading low-quality photos
- Ignoring the Q&A section
