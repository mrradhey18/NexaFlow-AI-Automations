Before you upload

A few quick guidelines

  • Upload original quality videos
  • Portrait videos preferred
  • Avoid WhatsApp compression — upload the original file
  • Upload multiple short clips rather than one long one
  • Mention the treatment or event in the notes
  • Include patient consent where applicable
