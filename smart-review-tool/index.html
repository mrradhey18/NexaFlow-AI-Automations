How the Smart Review QR System Works

Four frictionless steps turn a clinic visit into a published 5-star Google review — completely on autopilot.

01

Patient Scans QR

A branded QR card sits at your reception or on the table. Patient scans it instantly with any smartphone — no app needed.

02

Selects Service

A clean page asks which service they received — Consultation, Follow-up, Medicine, or Other. One tap selection.

SMART
03

Smart Rating Filter

Patient rates their experience. 3–5 stars → redirected to Google to publish. 1–2 stars → feedback captured privately, never reaches Google.

04

Review Published

Happy patients post their review directly on your Google Business Profile. More reviews = higher ranking = more new patients.

The Smart Filter — Your Reputation Shield

Patients leaving 2 stars or less are automatically routed to a private feedback form instead of Google. You learn what went wrong — without it damaging your public reputation. This is the feature that changes everything.
